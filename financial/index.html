What is the Dangote IPO?

Plain language.
No jargon.

An IPO (Initial Public Offering) means Dangote Industries is selling shares of the company to the public for the first time. Buying in means owning a piece of Nigeria's biggest business empire.

What is an IPO?

A company goes from privately owned to publicly traded. Shares are offered to investors at a set price before listing on the stock exchange. Once listed, you can buy or sell freely.

Sectors covered

Dangote Group spans five industries that underpin Nigeria's economy — giving investors exposure to infrastructure-grade assets previously reserved for institutional players.

Cement Petroleum refining Fertilisers Food processing Petrochemicals

The headline fact

The Dangote Refinery is the world's largest single-train petroleum refinery — with a capacity of 650,000 barrels per day. Nigeria's refining capacity deficit creates a structural demand floor for its output. This is not a speculative bet. It is a bet on infrastructure.

Retail investors can now access the same asset class that was previously only available to institutional players.
